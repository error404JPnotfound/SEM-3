Famous Places to Visit

Taj Mahal, Agra

One of the Seven Wonders of the World, the Taj Mahal is an ivory-white marble mausoleum built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Known as the "Pink City", Jaipur is famous for its stunning palaces, forts, and vibrant bazaars. Visit Hawa Mahal, Amber Fort, and City Palace.

Kerala Backwaters

Experience the serene backwaters of Kerala on a traditional houseboat. Surrounded by palm trees and lush greenery, it's truly "God's Own Country".

Leh-Ladakh

A paradise for adventure lovers. With breathtaking mountain passes, crystal-clear lakes like Pangong Tso, and ancient monasteries.

Varanasi

One of the oldest living cities in the world. The evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat is a spiritual experience you'll never forget.

Goa

India's beach capital! Known for its sandy shores, Portuguese architecture, vibrant nightlife, and delicious seafood cuisine.
