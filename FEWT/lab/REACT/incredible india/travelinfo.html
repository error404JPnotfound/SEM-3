How to Reach

  • By Train: Gujarat is well-connected by Western Railways. Major junctions include Ahmedabad (Kalupur), Vadodara, Surat, and Rajkot. High-speed trains like Vande Bharat, Shatabdi, and Rajdhani connect to Mumbai and Delhi.

  • By Bus: The GSRTC (Gujarat State Road Transport Corporation) operates extensive bus services connecting all villages and cities. Private luxury and sleeper buses are also available from neighboring states like Rajasthan and Maharashtra.

  • By Air: The Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (Ahmedabad) connects to major global cities. Domestic airports are available in Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot (Hirasar), Bhuj, and Jamnagar.





Local Transport

For travelling inside the city you can use the following options:

  • Auto-rickshaws and cycle-rickshaws (CNG autos are very common)
  • App-based taxi services (Uber/Ola available in major cities)
  • City buses at fixed routes (AMTS/BRTS services)
  • Rental bikes or bicycles

Rajasthan

How to Reach

  • By Train: Rajasthan is well-connected by Indian Railways. The "Palace on Wheels" offers a luxury experience. Major stations include Jaipur, Jodhpur, and Udaipur.
  • By Bus: RSRTC (Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation) and private luxury buses operate from Delhi, Gujarat, and other neighboring states.
  • By Air: Jaipur International Airport is the main gateway. Other domestic airports are located in Udaipur, Jodhpur, and Jaisalmer.

Local Transport

For travelling inside the cities of Rajasthan you can use the following options:

  • Auto-rickshaws and E-rickshaws (Tuk-tuks)
  • App-based taxi services (Uber/Ola are available in major cities like Jaipur and Udaipur)
  • Jaipur Metro (for travel within Jaipur city limits)
  • Rental bikes or Royal Enfield motorcycles

General Travel Tips

Please follow these basic tips during your journey in Rajasthan:

  1. Keep your identity proof and important documents safely.
  2. Wear modest clothing, especially when visiting temples or rural areas.
  3. Stay hydrated and carry sunscreen, as the desert sun can be harsh.
  4. Respect local culture and follow instructions at religious places.
  5. Avoid throwing plastic or waste at public places; keep the heritage clean.





Punjab

How to Reach

  • By Train: Well-connected rail network. Amritsar Junction and Ludhiana are major hubs. The Shatabdi Express connects New Delhi to Punjab efficiently.
  • By Bus: Punjab Roadways and private Volvo buses operate extensively from Delhi (ISBT) and neighboring states like Himachal and Haryana.
  • By Air: Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport (Amritsar) and Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport (Chandigarh) are the main airports.

Local Transport

For travelling inside the city you can use the following options:

  • Auto-rickshaws and E-rickshaws (common for short distances)
  • App-based taxi services (Ola/Uber available in Amritsar, Ludhiana, Chandigarh)
  • Hop-On Hop-Off Buses (available for tourists in Amritsar)
  • Cycle-rickshaws for narrow market streets

Karnataka

How to Reach

  • By Train: Karnataka is well-connected by Indian Railways. Major junctions include KSR Bengaluru (SBC), Yesvantpur, and Mysuru Junction.
  • By Bus: KSRTC (Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation) operates extensive services from neighboring states and within the state.
  • By Air: The primary gateway is Kempegowda International Airport (Bengaluru). Other key airports include Mangaluru, Mysuru, and Hubballi.

Local Transport

For travelling inside the city you can use the following options:

  • Auto-rickshaws (Metered in major cities like Bengaluru/Mysuru)
  • App-based taxi services (Ola, Uber, etc.)
  • BMTC (Bengaluru) and KSRTC city buses
  • Namma Metro (Bengaluru Metro Rail)

Uttarakhand

How to Reach

  • By Train: The main railway stations are Dehradun (DDN) for Garhwal and Kathgodam (KGM) for Kumaon regions.
  • By Bus: Uttarakhand Transport Corporation (UTC) and private Volvo buses run regularly from ISBT Delhi (Kashmere Gate).
  • By Air: The primary airport is Jolly Grant Airport (Dehradun). Pantnagar Airport serves the Kumaon region.

Local Transport

For travelling inside the state and hill stations, you can use the following options:

  • Shared Jeeps and Max (most common in hilly areas)
  • Private Taxis and rental cars
  • State-run buses for inter-city travel
  • Ponies or Palkis (specifically for pilgrimage treks like Kedarnath)

General Travel Tips

Please follow these basic tips during your journey in the mountains:

  1. Always carry light woolens, as weather in the hills can change rapidly even in summer.
  2. Carry sufficient cash, as ATMs may be scarce or out of order in remote high-altitude areas.
  3. If you have motion sickness, carry necessary medication for winding hill roads.
  4. Respect the sanctity of religious sites (Char Dham) and follow dress codes.
  5. Do not litter; plastic is banned in many ecological zones of the state.
